Château Coffee Co.
The Feature Story

Step through the door, leave the parking lot behind

From the outside, it is an ordinary stop on S Waverly Rd. Inside, Château Coffee Co. is a small, elegant room with a fireplace, a deep green wall and drinks built with care, like a French café that took a wrong turn and decided to stay in Lansing.

It is the place regulars, students and remote workers pick when they want something a cut above a chain, without losing the neighborhood feel that keeps them coming back.

Refined, never stuffy. A château you can visit in your sweatpants.
