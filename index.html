The Château

Elevated, but always approachable

Château Coffee Co. brings French-inspired charm to a busy Lansing road. It is the kind of place you choose on purpose instead of a chain: a cozy, photogenic room, a menu that changes with the seasons, and a loyal local following.

Neighbors, students, remote workers and families all find their corner here. Come for a quick espresso or settle in for the afternoon.

What we pour and bake

From the espresso bar to the pastry case

The espresso bar

Espresso and coffee

Lattes poured with care, classic espresso drinks and everyday coffee, made to be lingered over.

Changes with the seasons

Seasonal specialty drinks

A specialty menu that turns over through the year, plus coffee flights for trying a few favorites side by side.

From the pastry case

Pastries and sweets

Macarons in every color and pastries that belong beside a good cup, a true patisserie touch.

Something to share

Cakes by the slice

Layered slices like chocolate pistachio for an afternoon treat, or for splitting with a friend.

Something savory

Toasts and bowls

Light bites to pair with your coffee, like caprese toast with mozzarella, tomato and balsamic, or a fruit-topped bowl with granola.

Stay a while

A cozy room for gathering

Green walls, a fireplace and plenty of seats. Bring a laptop, a book or the whole family. This is your charming little escape on the south side of town.
